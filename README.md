# HNF1A variants and maturity-onset diabetes of the young in Jammu

**Ivrandeep Kour · MSc Biotechnology · University of Jammu · 2023**  
School of Biotechnology | Supervisor: Dr. Nisha Kapoor

## Research question

For my MSc thesis, I investigated whether two variants in the *HNF1A* gene, rs1169305 and rs2464195, were associated with maturity-onset diabetes of the young (MODY) in a study population in Jammu, India. I compared genetic data from participants with MODY and healthy controls.

## My contribution

- Helped collect study samples, extracted DNA, and checked its quality before PCR.
- Designed primers with Primer3 and checked their specificity with UCSC In-Silico PCR.
- Interpreted Sanger sequencing results for genotyping. Used Excel for data handling and chi-square analysis, and MedCalc to estimate an odds ratio and confidence interval.

## What I found

For **rs2464195**, the A allele was more frequent in the MODY group than in the control group. The thesis reported an allelic odds ratio of **3.9** (95% confidence interval **2.16–7.02**, Table 17). I interpreted this as a preliminary association in the study sample. The small sample limits how broadly the result can be applied, and an independent cohort would be needed to assess whether the finding holds.

For **rs1169305**, sequencing results were pending when I submitted the thesis, so I did not draw an association conclusion for that variant.
